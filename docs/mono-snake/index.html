MONO SNAKE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

MONO SNAKE is an offline arcade game developed by Oleksandr Matvieiev.

Personal data

MONO SNAKE does not require user accounts. The app does not collect, send, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Local information

The game saves high scores and user preferences, such as sound, vibration, and language choices, locally on your device using Android app storage. This information is not transmitted to the developer or third parties. Uninstalling the app normally removes this locally stored information.

Permissions and third-party services

The current version of MONO SNAKE does not request internet access or sensitive Android permissions and does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or social media services.

Retention and deletion

Local high scores and preferences remain on your device until you reset the app data or uninstall the app. You can delete them at any time through your device settings by clearing MONO SNAKE app storage, or by uninstalling the app. We have no server-side user records to retain or delete.

Children's privacy

MONO SNAKE does not offer user accounts, chat, or user-generated content. It does not knowingly collect data from children or adults.

Updates to this policy

If the game's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the app's Google Play Data safety information will be reviewed.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer, Oleksandr Matvieiev, at matveevoleksandr83@gmail.com.
